# Failure Modes

- the late-only gate still activates too often and recreates broad recovery regressions
- secondary recovery adds movement but does not improve score or order completion
- redirected bots increase congestion near shelves or dropoff lanes
- starvation proxies improve only because bots move more, not because assignments recover meaningfully
- the gate is so narrow that the mechanism almost never activates and the experiment is effectively a no-op
- offline behavior and live behavior diverge enough that the causal story is not trustworthy
